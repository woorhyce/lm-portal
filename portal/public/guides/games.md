# LM Arcade: DreamCatcher Replay Academy

An arcade-style quiz for learning DreamCatcher replay operation. Good for new operators, and for a quick refresher before a show.

## How to play

Each question shows four answers under **CAM A** to **CAM D**. A gate scrolls toward you: move your camera under the right answer before it arrives.

- **← →** or tap a camera to move.
- **Space** or tap the same camera again to lock in early for a **speed bonus**.
- Right answers in a row build a **streak**. Wrong answers cost a life.

## The 7 stages

1. Power Up
2. On Air
3. Replay Theory
4. Find It Fast
5. Playlists
6. Bins & Export
7. Troubleshoot

You start with three lives. On **Game Over**, **Continue** restarts the current stage with three fresh lives, and your score rolls back to where it was at the start of that stage.

## Notes

- Your high score follows you to any computer you log in from.
- **SND ON** toggles sound.

# LM Arcade: Sixteen

A speed-typing game. Sixteen blank slots, no hints: type the word as fast as you can and press **Enter**.

- The clock starts on your first key and stops when you press **Enter**.
- Letters show as dots, never as characters.
- A wrong word sets off the fail screen, clears the slots, and the clock keeps running.
- Under 1.5 s you're the best, 1.5–3 s is fine, over 3 s gets roasted. A new team record gets the **HIGH SCORE!!!!** screen.
- The team leaderboard keeps each person's best time. **Sound on/off** sits next to **New run**.
