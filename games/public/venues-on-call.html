One week on call

Tickets roll in from venues all week. Pick a ticket and have someone remote in. Once they’re connected, open the session: read the logs, run commands, and pick the fix. Stuck? Phone a friend on the team. Or let them work it alone, which is slower and they can guess wrong.

Most problems get fixed remotely. When it’s hardware (a drive, a Karak or Matrox card, RAM, a panel), they grab the part and get on a plane. Close tickets before the customer runs out of patience, especially P1s with a show tonight. Time runs at a quarter speed while a session is open.

Keys: ↑ ↓ picks a ticket, 1 to 5 remotes that teammate in (or opens their session), Enter opens the next session waiting, Q uses the teammate’s special, P pauses. In a session: type commands (Tab completes, “help” lists them), 1 to 4 picks an answer, Esc steps out.
